D. Answer in about 4 sentences (2 marks each)

1.Give the meaning of Short-run and Long-run concepts.

Model Question · P2-Q19Question Bank · V.1
Answer

Short run: In the short run, at least one of the factors – labour or capital – cannot be varied, and therefore remains fixed. In order to vary the output level, the firm can vary only the other factor. The factor that remains fixed is called the fixed factor, and the factor which the firm can vary is called the variable factor.

Long run: In the long run, all factors of production can be varied. A firm may vary both the inputs simultaneously. So, in the long run, there is no fixed factor.

We define a period as long run or short run simply by looking at whether all the inputs can be varied or not, and not in terms of days, months or years.

2.State the types of Returns to scale.

Model Question · P5-Q19Question Bank · V.2
Answer
  1. Constant Returns to Scale (CRS): When a proportional increase in all inputs results in an increase in output by the same proportion.
  2. Increasing Returns to Scale (IRS): When a proportional increase in all inputs results in an increase in output by a larger proportion.
  3. Decreasing Returns to Scale (DRS): When a proportional increase in all inputs results in an increase in output by a smaller proportion.

Example: If all inputs are doubled and output gets doubled, it is CRS; if output is more than doubled, it is IRS; if output is less than doubled, it is DRS.

3.Name any two Short run costs.

Model Question · P1-Q19Question Bank · V.3
Answer
  1. Total Fixed Cost (TFC): The cost that a firm incurs to employ the fixed inputs. Whatever amount of output the firm produces, this cost remains fixed.
  2. Total Variable Cost (TVC): The cost that a firm incurs to employ the variable inputs. As output increases, total variable cost increases.

4.What are Long run costs?

Model Question · P3-Q19Question Bank · V.4
Answer

In the long run, all inputs are variable. There are no fixed costs. The total cost and the total variable cost therefore coincide in the long run.

  1. Long run average cost (LRAC) is the cost per unit of output: LRAC = TC / q
  2. Long run marginal cost (LRMC) is the change in total cost per unit of change in output: LRMC = (TC at q1 units) – (TC at q1 – 1 units)

In the long run, the sum of all marginal costs up to some output level gives us the total cost at that level.

4.Write about returns to scale.

Question Bank · VI.4Teacher's Notes · I.3
Answer

A. Increasing returns to scale (IRS): When a proportional increase in all inputs results in an increase in output by a larger proportion is called as Increasing Returns to Scale. Eg: a 10 percent increase in all factor input results in 20 percent increase in total output.

B. Constant returns to scale (IRS): When a proportional increase in all inputs results in an increase in output by the same proportion is called as Constant Returns to Scale. Eg: a 10 percent increase in all factor input results in 10 percent increase in total output.

C. Decreasing Returns to scale (DRS): When a proportional increase in all inputs results in an increase in output by a smaller proportion is called as Decreasing Returns to Scale. Eg: a 10 percent increase in all factor input results in 5 percent increase in total output.

4.Explain the various Short run costs.

Question Bank · VII.4Teacher's Notes · II.1
Answer

a) Total Fixed cost (TFC)

Fixed cost is payment made to the fixed factors. It does not change with the changes in the level of output. It remains fixed. For example: - Rent of factory, insurance premium

b) Total variable cost (TVC)

Total variable cost is the payment made to the variable factors of Production. It changes with the changes in the level of output. For example: - wages of temporary labors, Electricity.

c) Total cost (TC)

Adding the both the fixed and variable costs, we get the total cost (TC) of a firm.
It can be written as TC= TFC+TVC

d) Average fixed cost (AFC)

Average fixed cost is the per unit fixed cost of production of a commodity.

AFC = TFCQ

AFC = average fixed cost   TFC = Total Fixed Cost
Q = No. of Units Produced

e) Average variable cost (AVC)

It is per unit variable cost of production of a commodity. We can obtain AVC by dividing the total variable cost by the number of units produced.

AVC = TVCQ

AVC = Average variable cost TVC = Total Variable Cost
Q = No. of Units Produced.

f) Short run Average cost (SAC)

Average cost is the per unit total cost of production. So,

AC = TCQ

AC = Average Cost   TC = Total Cost
Q = No. of Units Produced

g) Short run Marginal cost (SMC)

Marginal cost is the cost of producing extra unit of a commodity. It is the additional to total cost by the production of an additional unit of the commodity. So,

MC = TCn-TCn-1   MC = Marginal Cost

TCn= Total cost of 'n' units, TCn-1 = Total Cost of n-1 units

2.Explain the meaning of TP, MP and AP. (For Blind students only)

Question Bank · VIII (Blind students)Teacher's Notes · III.1
Answer

Total Product (TP): Suppose we vary a single input and keep all other inputs constant. Then for different levels of that input, we get different levels of output. This relationship between the variable input and output, keeping all other inputs constant, is referred to as Total Product (TP) of the variable input. It is also called total return or total physical product of the variable input.

Average Product (AP): Average product is defined as the output per unit of variable input.
APL = TPLL

Marginal Product (MP): Marginal product of an input is defined as the change in output per unit of change in the input when all other inputs are held constant.
MPL = ΔTPLΔL

Total product is the sum of marginal products, and average product at any level of employment is the average of all marginal products up to that level.
